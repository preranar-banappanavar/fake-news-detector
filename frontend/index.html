ABOUT TURBIT

Turbit is an AI-powered Truth Engine designed to identify misinformation patterns in news headlines and article bodies.

TECHNICAL DETAILS

Built with a FastAPI backend and a Scikit-Learn pipeline (TF-IDF vectorizer + Logistic Regression classifier) trained on a 20,000 subset of the WELFake dataset.

ACCURACY

Our optimized lightweight model achieves a test set accuracy of 95.9%, ensuring highly reliable predictive confidence ratings.
